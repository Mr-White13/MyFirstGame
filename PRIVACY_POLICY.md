## 1. Summary

- NEON ESCAPE has **no accounts** and **no servers of its own**. Your progress, statistics,
  achievements, settings, Energy Credits and cosmetics are stored **only on your device**.
- Mr. White Games does **not** receive, collect or sell your personal data.
- The Android app can show **optional rewarded ads** from **Google AdMob**, only when you tap to
  watch one. When ads are used, Google collects some data as described in section 4.
- Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and
  Switzerland), Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you
  can change it at any time in **Settings → Privacy options (ads)**.

## 2. Data stored on your device (not sent to us)

The game saves the following in the app's local storage on your device so you can continue
playing:

- Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, best times, an unfinished level you can resume.
- Statistics and personal records (for example levels completed, play time, mistakes).
- Achievements, daily reward streak, Daily League results and Energy Points.
- Energy Credits, boosters used and cosmetic items (avatar frames) you unlocked.
- Your profile: a nickname (generated or chosen by you), an avatar, and an optional country
  flag you can choose to display.
- Settings: theme, sound, vibration, language and accessibility preferences.

This data never leaves your device through the game. It is not used to identify you, and
Mr. White Games has no access to it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its
storage. ⚠ Depending on your device settings, Android may include app data in **your own**
device backup (Google account backup); this is controlled by you and by Google, not by
Mr. White Games.

The country you may choose is only a flag shown on your profile; the game does not determine
your location.

## 3. Rewarded ads (Android app only)

The Android app offers optional **rewarded ads**: short videos you can choose to watch in
exchange for in-game Energy Credits or to continue a level after losing all lives.

- Ads are **only shown after you tap** a "watch ad" button. The game never shows ads
  automatically.
- You never have to watch ads to play; every level can be played without them.
- Rewards are in-game items only. They have no real-world or monetary value.
- The web version of NEON ESCAPE shows no ads.

Ads are provided by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and consent is managed by Google's
**User Messaging Platform** (UMP).

## 4. Data collected by Google (third-party SDK)

When the ad SDK is active, Google — not Mr. White Games — collects and shares the following
data automatically, according to Google's own disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, for
**advertising, analytics and fraud prevention** purposes:

- **IP address**, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device.
- **Product interactions**, such as app launches, taps and video views.
- **Diagnostic information**, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
- **Device and account identifiers**, such as the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and,
  where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google states that this data is **encrypted in transit** (TLS). Google processes it under its
own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google's disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK:
  https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choices and your device settings.

## 5. Your choices

- **Don't watch ads.** No ad is shown unless you tap to watch one.
- **Consent.** Where required, Google's consent form lets you accept, refuse or manage
  purposes. If you refuse, the game keeps working; ads may be unavailable or non-personalised.
  Change your choice any time in **Settings → Privacy options (ads)** (shown when Google's
  consent system requires it in your region).
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings
  (Settings → Privacy → Ads, wording varies by device).
- **Delete game data.** Uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android's settings. Resetting
  Settings inside the game only resets preferences, not progress.

## 6. Data retention

- Game data on your device stays until you delete it (uninstall or clear storage).
- Mr. White Games stores no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.
- Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies (see section 4 links).

## 8. Your rights (EEA / Romania)

Because Mr. White Games does not collect or hold your personal data, there is no personal data
of yours for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google in connection with
ads, you can exercise your rights with Google (see https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
change your consent as described in section 5. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with
your data protection authority; in Romania this is the **ANSPDCP** (Autoritatea Națională de
Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal, https://www.dataprotection.ro).

## 9. Security

The game stores its data in the app's private storage on your device. Ad-related data is sent
by Google's SDK over encrypted connections (TLS).
