      Daily challenges

        How the league works

        • Each day has 3 challenges with 4 Energy Arrows each. Clearing an Energy Arrow, by tapping it or by a chain reaction, earns 1 Energy Point, once per arrow per day: up to 12.
        • When a new day starts, the previous day is scored against your league's targets. Reach the promotion target to move up one league; score below the relegation threshold to move down one. Anything in between keeps your league.
        • You take part in a day by earning at least 1 point. The first day in a row without points is forgiven; each further one moves you down one league, never below Bronze.
        • These are personal, offline targets. There are no other players in this league yet, and no rankings.
