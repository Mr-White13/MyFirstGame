### Short description

### Full description

```
Clear the board, one arrow at a time.

NEON ESCAPE is a calm but clever logic puzzle. Every board is packed with glowing arrows. Tap an arrow and it slides off the board along its own path – if nothing is in its way. Pick the wrong one and it crashes into a neighbour, turns red and costs you a heart. Find the right order, free every arrow and escape.

SIMPLE TO LEARN, HARD TO MASTER
• One rule: an arrow leaves only when its path is clear.
• Three hearts per level. Fewer mistakes means more stars.
• Red arrows remember your tap: free their path and they leave on their own, setting off chain reactions.
• Quick, clean taps fill a streak bonus that clears two arrows for you.

100 HANDCRAFTED LEVELS
• Five chapters, from "It's Just the Beginning" to "The Last Escape".
• Boards grow from small warm-ups to huge, tangled mazes with pinch-to-zoom.
• Every level is checked to be solvable.
• Earn up to three stars per level and chase your best times.

DAILY LEAGUE
• After level 10, play three new daily challenges every day.
• Collect glowing Energy Arrows to earn Energy Points.
• Your daily score moves you up or down through six leagues, from Bronze to Neon Elite.

REWARDS AND STYLE
• 65 achievements, daily rewards and login streaks.
• Earn Energy Credits by playing and spend them on boosters – Hint and Reveal – when you're stuck.
• Unlock avatar frames in the Shop and build your player profile with avatars, records and statistics.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Plays offline. Your progress stays on your device – no account needed.
• Three themes: Dark Neon, Light and Colorful.
• Reduced motion, adjustable effects, sound and vibration settings.
• Available in English, Spanish and Romanian.
• Resume an unfinished level right where you left it.

Free to play. The game contains optional rewarded ads: watch one only if you want extra Energy Credits or a second chance after losing all hearts. You never need to watch an ad to play any level.

NEON ESCAPE is an original game by Mr. White Games.
```
